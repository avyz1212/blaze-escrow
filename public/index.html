BlazeEscrow

Trustless escrow for peer-to-peer and agent-to-agent payments on Bitcoin Cash. A CashScript covenant holds the money — not a company, not a middleman. It can only ever pay the seller on release, or the buyer on refund. Nobody can redirect it. Ever.

Demo mode: local mock network. The same compiled contract is evaluated by the real BCH script VM — rejections below are the covenant speaking. Chipnet/mainnet run the identical code via Electrum (see README).

    How it works

    1. Buyer, seller and arbiter agree on a price and a deadline. Those four values create the contract address.
    2. The buyer sends the payment to the contract address. The money is now locked by Bitcoin Cash script.
    3. Release: buyer or arbiter signs → the covenant forces payment to the seller.
    4. Refund: arbiter signs anytime, or the buyer signs after the deadline → the covenant forces payment back to the buyer.
    5. Anything else — wrong signer, wrong recipient, too-early refund — is rejected by the network's script rules. Try it above.

    Why it matters: AI agents and strangers on the internet cannot open bank accounts or sue each other. A covenant escrow gives them a neutral, programmable cashier that settles in seconds for a fraction of a cent — exactly what Bitcoin Cash was built for.
